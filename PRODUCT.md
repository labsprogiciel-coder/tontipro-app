# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Field agents who collect tontine savings door-to-door in francophone West Africa (FCFA, French-first). They record deposits and withdrawals for many clients a day, often offline, standing up, one-handed on a phone or TPE, in bright outdoor light, sometimes with the client watching the screen. Supervisors and admins use a lighter set of screens (reports, agents) on the same app; the Kauri web dashboard is their main tool.

## Product Purpose

TontiPro (branded Kauridorr) lets an agent find a client, record a collection or withdrawal in seconds, and trust that it syncs later when the network returns. Success is a fast, error-free collection round: no lost or doubled amounts, balances the agent can show the client with confidence, and reports that match what was collected.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, calm, efficient. A money-handling tool that feels sober and dependable rather than playful: clear numbers, short French labels, no surprises. The warm orange identity stays as the accent that marks actions and deposits, not as decoration.

## Anti-references

- Dense back-office or banking UIs: cramped tables, tiny text, many fields per screen.
- Flashy consumer apps: decorative gradients, glass effects, animation for its own sake on money screens.

## Design Principles

1. **Built for the field.** Every screen must work one-handed, standing, in sunlight, on a small screen and a slow or absent network.
2. **Numbers first.** Amounts, balances and counts are the content; labels support them, never compete with them.
3. **Calm confidence.** Sober, consistent layouts so agents build muscle memory; the same thing always sits in the same place with the same spacing.
4. **Show state honestly.** Offline, pending and rejected are always visible and never ambiguous.
5. **Say less.** Short French labels, icons and numbers over sentences.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.2 AA contrast minimum, tuned for outdoor sunlight readability.
- Touch targets at least 44pt; primary actions reachable one-handed.
- Low-literacy friendly: rely on icons, numbers and short French copy; don't depend on long text.
- Respect reduced-motion settings; no motion required to understand state.
